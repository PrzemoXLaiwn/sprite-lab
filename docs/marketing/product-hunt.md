## First maker comment (relaunch story)

```
Hi Product Hunt! I'm the solo developer behind SpriteLab.

Honest backstory: SpriteLab existed before, and it wasn't good. The old generator often ignored your prompt – you'd ask for a knight and get something only loosely related. I let the site sit for a while, and then decided to rebuild it from scratch instead of patching it.

What the new SpriteLab does:
• Text prompt → game-ready 2D sprites as transparent PNGs, in 12 art styles
• Pixel art that sits on a real pixel grid with a limited palette (no blurry "fake pixel" look)
• Animate any sprite: idle, walk, run, attack, jump, fly, turnaround or a custom motion
• It suggests motions that fit the sprite – a dog gets "wag tail" and "bark", a car gets "drive" and "drift", a dragon gets "fire breath"
• 4/6/9 key poses, or smooth mode up to 18 frames with AI in-betweens
• Export as sprite sheet, separate frames and GIF
• Seamless floor/wall tiles, animated water, icons
• Projects with automatic folder sorting + ZIP download, free pixel-perfect upscale

Pricing: 3 tries without an account, 10 free credits when you sign up (no card), plans from £5/month. Commercial use is allowed. If you used the old SpriteLab, log in again – returning users get +15 credits until 31 Dec 2026.

What it doesn't do (yet): no API, no 3D, no 8-direction sets, no autotile tilesets. I'd rather tell you now than have you find out.

I'd really love feedback from people who actually make games: which animation or asset type should I improve next?
```
